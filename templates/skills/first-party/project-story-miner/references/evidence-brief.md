## Contradictions and gaps

Record conflicting numbers, missing dates, ambiguous motivations, and claims that need a stronger source. Do not choose the convenient version silently.

## Publication and privacy review

List details to redact, generalize, verify, attribute, or obtain permission for. State `none found` only after checking.

## Story candidates

For each candidate include the reader promise, central claim, opening moment, strongest evidence, proof boundary, missing author detail, and recommended format.

## Focused author questions

Ask only for memories, judgments, or context the artifacts cannot supply, such as what changed the author's mind or why one tradeoff felt acceptable.

## Handoff

Name the recommended angle, required facts, exact terminology, limitations, and source paths for the drafting stage.
