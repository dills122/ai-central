# Voice Calibration

Prefer a supplied author sample. Extract its rhythm and choices; do not imitate private facts or transplant memorable phrases into unrelated posts.

Without a sample, use a restrained technical voice:

- serious about mechanisms, evidence, and tradeoffs
- conversational without chatty filler
- skeptical of vanity metrics and hand-waving
- willing to state a supported judgment plainly
- precise about what evidence proves and does not prove
- mildly dry only when the engineering situation earns it
- more interested in the work than self-promotion

Mix short conclusions with longer explanations. Use first person only when supported. Prefer plain words to management or marketing vocabulary. Do not end every section with a slogan.

Treat phrases and constructions as repetition signals, not banned words. In particular, watch repeated uses of `This matters because`, `The lesson is`, binary `not X but Y` contrasts, rhetorical questions, and perfectly symmetrical paragraphs.
